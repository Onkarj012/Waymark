Scope

  • Checkout Core API, edge routing, shadow comparison, dashboards, and rollback controls.
  • Contract, timeout, duplicate-submit, reconciliation, and cutover verification.
  • No payment-provider replacement, UI redesign, pricing change, or order-database split.

Requirements and design

Every request carries a correlation ID and payment attempt key. Shadow mode compares normalized outcomes without issuing a second authorization; the same key makes retries safe across the cutover boundary.

Phase 01 — parity and shadow

Outcome: Core produces equivalent normalized outcomes for one full business day without duplicate payment authorization.

  • Deploy the internal Core route.
  • Compare status, order state, and normalized provider outcomes.
  • Stop on unexplained mismatch before read exposure.

Risks and mitigations

  • High risk
    Duplicate authorizationCarry one attempt key, prohibit shadow authorization, and alert on provider idempotency conflicts.
  • Medium risk
    Order divergenceCompare normalized transitions and block traffic increases on any unexplained mismatch.
  • Medium risk
    Rollback gapDrain in-flight requests, replay the outbox, and verify reconciliation before restoring writes.
  • Low risk
    Provider timeout sensitivityMeasure p95 and p99 at every gate and retain the monolith route for rollback.
